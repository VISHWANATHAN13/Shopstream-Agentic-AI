ShopStream India Customer Support Knowledge Base

Orders:
Customers can cancel an order before it is shipped.
Once an order has been shipped, cancellation is generally unavailable.
Customers can track shipped orders using their order ID.

Refunds:
Refunds are processed after the returned product is received and inspected.
Approved refunds usually take 5 to 7 business days to appear in the customer's original payment method.

Damaged Products:
Customers who receive a damaged product should report the issue within 48 hours of delivery.
Customers should provide photographs of the damaged product and packaging.
The support team may offer a replacement or refund depending on the case.

Payments:
If a payment succeeds but the order is not created, customers should wait a few minutes and check their order history.
If the amount was deducted but no order exists, the payment team should investigate the transaction.
