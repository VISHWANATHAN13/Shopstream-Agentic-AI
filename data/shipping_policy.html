ShopStream India Shipping Policy

Standard Shipping

Standard shipping is available for most products across India. Orders are normally delivered within 3 to 7 business days after dispatch.

Express Shipping

Express shipping is available for selected products and locations. Express orders are normally delivered within 1 to 3 business days after dispatch.

Order Tracking

Customers can track their orders using the tracking number provided after the order has been dispatched.

Delayed Orders

If an order has not arrived within the expected delivery period, customers should contact ShopStream support with their order ID.

Delivery Address

Customers should verify their delivery address before placing an order. Changes to the delivery address may not be possible after the order has been shipped.

Failed Delivery

If delivery fails because the customer is unavailable or the address cannot be verified, the courier may attempt delivery again.
